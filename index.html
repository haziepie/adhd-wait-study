Are you waiting for an ADHD assessment for your child?

Most research looks at what happens after a diagnosis. This study is about the time spent waiting, and what that time is actually like for parents.

  • One interview of about an hour
  • Online on Microsoft Teams, at a time that suits you
  • Your name is never used

Who can take part

Parents and carers in England whose primary-school-aged child (about 5 to 11) has been referred for an ADHD assessment and is on the waiting list.

To find out more, email Hazel Yeo and you will be sent an information sheet. Asking does not commit you to anything.

Email Hazel

Or write to: hy23819@essex.ac.uk

A student research study at the University of Essex Online, with ethical approval. There is no payment. It is not a test, and it takes no view on diagnosis or medication. Parents and carers must be 18 or over, and the child must not already have an ADHD diagnosis. Not open to people who know the researcher personally. This page does not collect any information about you.
